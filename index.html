About Tank Lore

Tank Lore is stylized action rogue-lite bullet-hell dungeon crawler that can be played solo or co-op where you play as legendary tank heroes fighting in a rebellion against an evil empire.

Fight against Evil

As one of the hero tanks you'll wield the powers and weapons of the Legendary Shells to fight against Zeroth's Null Core Empire as you grow stronger and unlock new powers and abilities. Mix and match core abilities with powerful upgrades liberated from Zeroth's Evil Army!

Wield legendary power!

Move the story forward and unlock new playable hero tanks that wield different and powerful Legendary Shells, each with their unique set of powers, abilities, upgrades, and possible synergies!

Try again and again!

No run is as the last one, as new paths become available. Use the resources you acquire to unlock Modifications that give you a leg up next time to try your mettle against the forces of Zeroth.
